# Act 1: The Afternoon: بعد الضهر

**Full production script: 15–20 minutes of play**
**Time:** 3:00 PM – 4:15 PM, August 2014
**Setting:** A besieged town in eastern Ghouta, outskirts of Damascus

---

## SCENE 1: THE WALK

**Location:** شارع الزيتون / Zeitoun Street: eastern residential quarter
**Time:** 3:05 PM
**Lighting:** Harsh August sun, high and white. Deep black shadows from damaged apartment blocks. Dust suspended in the air catches the light in visible shafts. Overexposed patches on sunlit concrete; near-darkness under overhangs and in doorways.
**Ambient sound:** Distant sporadic gunfire (single shots, irregular: a sniper exchange somewhere to the south). A generator humming from inside a building. Pigeons startled from a ledge. Wind through broken windows, a low whistle. Somewhere, metal scraping, someone working on something unseen.

### Gameplay

**[TUTORIAL SEQUENCE]**

The player controls Sami. He walks forward along a narrow residential street. Ahmad walks beside him, slightly ahead, his pace loose and unhurried. The camera is side-on, tracking them.

**Controls introduced:**
- Move (walk forward/backward)
- Look at / examine (interaction button)

The street is flanked by four- and five-storey apartment buildings, most with façades partially torn open by previous shelling. Rebar juts from concrete like broken bones. A balcony hangs at an angle, laundry still on the line, sun-bleached, forgotten. The street is empty of other people.

A **grape vine** grows from a crack in the rubble on the right side, climbing the remains of a garden wall. Leaves are dusty but green. Small clusters of unripe grapes hang from it.

**[INTERACTION: GRAPE VINE]**

> **Arabic:** دالية عنب. لسا عم تطلع من بين الحجار. ما حدا قلّها إنو في حرب.
> **English:** A grape vine. Still growing up through the stones. No one told it there's a war.

### Dialogue

*Ahmad and Sami walk. Ahmad is half a step ahead. He speaks first, casually, as though continuing a conversation that didn't need to be started.*

**أحمد:**
> عارف شو أكتر شي بشتاقلو؟
> *You know what I miss most?*

*Beat. Sami glances at him.*

**سامي:**
> الكهربا.
> *Electricity.*

**أحمد:**
> لا يا زلمة. البوظة. بوظة بكداش.
> *No, man. Ice cream. Bakdash ice cream.*

**سامي:**
> *(half-smiling)* من كل شي عم يصير، البوظة؟
> *(half-smiling)* Out of everything that's happening, ice cream?

**أحمد:**
> إي والله. هديك البوظة العربية بالفستق الحلبي. بتذكّر كنّا نروح أنا وياك عالحميدية ونوقف بالطابور ربع ساعة؟
> *I swear. That Arabic ice cream with Aleppo pistachios. Remember we used to go to the Hamidiyya and queue for fifteen minutes?*

**سامي:**
> نص ساعة.
> *Half an hour.*

**أحمد:**
> بتستاهل كل دقيقة.
> *Worth every minute.*

*A pause. They walk. A pigeon bursts from a window above them. Both flinch slightly, a reflex.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The flinch is automatic, shared, unremarked. The body learns the siege before the mind does. Neither of them acknowledges it.]*

**سامي:**
> كيف الولاد اليوم؟
> *How were the kids today?*

**أحمد:**
> منيحين. عم يتعلّمو ماضي وحاضر ومستقبل.
> *Good. They're learning past, present and future.*

**سامي:**
> بالقواعد تقصد.
> *You mean in grammar.*

**أحمد:**
> *(smiling)* هيك قلت.
> *(smiling)* That's what I said.

*[ACTING NOTE: Ahmad's smile carries a double meaning he half-intends. The students learn tenses. The town has a past, a present, and a future, maybe. He means both.]*

**أحمد:**
> بتعرف رامي؟ ابن أبو علي؟ اللي ساكنين بآخر الشارع؟
> *You know Rami? Abu Ali's son? The ones who live at the end of the street?*

**سامي:**
> الصغير؟ اللي ما بيوقف حكي؟
> *The little one? Who never stops talking?*

**أحمد:**
> هاد. سألني اليوم: أستاز، شو يعني "برّا"؟
> *That's him. He asked me today: "Teacher, what does 'abroad' mean?"*

*Sami is quiet.*

**أحمد:**
> يعني ما بيعرف. ما طلع من هون بحياتو. ما شاف شي غير هالحارة. "برّا" بالنسبالو كلمة مثل "تنّين": شي بيسمع فيه بس ما شافو.
> *He doesn't know. He's never been outside this neighbourhood in his life. He's never seen anything else. "Abroad" is like "dragon" to him: something he's heard of but never seen.*

*[ACTING NOTE: This lands on Sami. He doesn't respond immediately. The silence is the response.]*

### Gameplay: The Sniper Curtain

They approach a gap between two buildings. Across the gap, a **sniper curtain**, a large bedsheet and two blankets, stitched together and strung on wire between the buildings at head height. It blocks the line of sight from the western ridge where regime snipers are positioned.

**[INTERACTION: SNIPER CURTAIN]**

> **Arabic:** ستارة قنّاص. شراشف مشدودة بين البنايتين. الطلقة بتفوت منها بس القنّاص ما بيشوف مين بيمشي وراها.
> **English:** A sniper curtain. Sheets strung between the buildings. The bullet goes through, but the sniper can't see who's walking behind it.

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT]** The player must crouch to pass under a section where the curtain has sagged, its support wire cut by shrapnel. A prompt appears:

> **[Crouch]**: وطّي / Crouch down

*[TUTORIAL: Crouching introduced. The player ducks under the sagging curtain. Ahmad does the same beside them.]*

**أحمد:**
> *(ducking)* يا ريت كانت بوظة بكداش بتحمي من القنّاص.
> *(ducking)* If only Bakdash ice cream could protect you from snipers.

**سامي:**
> لو هيك كانت أغلى من الدهب.
> *If it could, it'd be worth more than gold.*

### Gameplay: The Collapsed Wall

They continue. Ahead, part of a building has collapsed across the street, concrete slabs and rubble form a waist-high barrier.

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT]** The player must jump/climb over the rubble.

> **[Jump + Climb]**: نطّ / Jump, اطلع / Climb

*[TUTORIAL: Jumping and climbing introduced. Sami clambers over. Ahmad follows, steadying himself on a piece of rebar.]*

On the other side, the street opens slightly. To the left, the remains of a **small shop**, shelves gone, stripped to the walls, the metal shutter half-closed and dented.

**[INTERACTION: EMPTY SHOP]**

> **Arabic:** دكّان أبو ريّان. كان يبيع كل شي: خبز وسكّر وسجاير. هلّق ما فيه غير غبرة.
> **English:** Abu Rayyan's shop. It used to sell everything: bread, sugar, cigarettes. Now there's nothing in it but dust.

*[ACTING NOTE: Sami's examination text is factual, not sentimental. He has passed this shop a thousand times. The emptiness is normal now. That normalcy is the point.]*

## SCENE 2: THE PARTING

**Location:** تقاطع الحارة / The neighbourhood junction: where Zeitoun Street meets the road south
**Time:** 3:18 PM
**Lighting:** Same harsh sun. The junction is more open: more exposed. Shadows are shorter here. A damaged mosque is visible at the far end of the southern road, its minaret cracked but standing.
**Ambient sound:** The generator is behind them now, fainter. A child's voice calling from inside a building: indistinct, ordinary. The distant gunfire has paused.

### Dialogue

*They reach the junction. The road splits: east continues toward Sami's neighbourhood and the water infrastructure he tends; south goes toward the frontline quarter where Ahmad is heading to check on a family.*

**أحمد:**
> *(stopping)* يلا، أنا رايح من هون. بدي مرّ عند أم سعيد وعيلتا، بنتن مريضة وما في حدا يشوفها.
> *(stopping)* Right, I'm heading off from here. I want to check on Um Said and her family. Their daughter's ill and there's no one to see her.

**سامي:**
> من وين رايح؟ من شارع المدرسة؟
> *Which way are you going? Through School Street?*

**أحمد:**
> إي، أسرع.
> *Yeah, it's quicker.*

*[ACTING NOTE: School Street runs through the southern quarter near the frontline. It's faster but more exposed. Sami knows this. He considers saying something.]*

**سامي:**
> خلّي بالك ع حالك.
> *Take care of yourself.*

**أحمد:**
> *(lightly)* دايماً. بشوفك بالليل إن شاء الله. عندي نكتة جديدة، مو رح تصدّقها.
> *(lightly)* Always. I'll see you tonight, God willing. I've got a new joke. You won't believe it.

**سامي:**
> نكاتك ما بتنضحك.
> *Your jokes aren't funny.*

**أحمد:**
> *(grinning)* هاي الأحلى من كلهن. الليلة بتسمعها.
> *(grinning)* This one's the best yet. Tonight, you'll hear it.

*[ACTING NOTE: Ahmad's grin is real, full, unguarded. This is the last time Sami sees his face. The moment must feel completely ordinary, two friends parting after a walk, a joke deferred. The player should feel the ordinariness. It's only later that ordinariness becomes unbearable.]*

**أحمد:**
> يلا، يا هندسة.
> *See you, ya handasa.*

*Ahmad raises a hand, a half-wave, casual, and walks south. Sami watches him. Ahmad doesn't look back.*

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT]** The player cannot follow. The camera holds on Sami watching Ahmad walk down the southern road. Ahmad's figure grows smaller. He passes behind a damaged building, reappears briefly on the other side, then turns a corner.

The street is empty.

**Sound:** A beat of silence. Then the generator hum fades back in. A pigeon coos. The ordinary world reasserts itself over the space where Ahmad was.

*The player regains control. Sami stands at the junction alone.*

## SCENE 3: THE HOUR

**Location:** Various: eastern quarter streets, moving north-east
**Time:** 3:22 PM – 4:05 PM
**Lighting:** Still bright, but the sun has shifted. Shadows are growing longer on the western sides of buildings. The light is less white, beginning its slow turn toward amber. Dust in the air catches the angled light.
**Ambient sound:** The ambient environment deepens. More layered: distant shelling (thuds, rhythmic, from the south-west: regime positions firing on another part of Ghouta), the walkie-talkie static from a building, a baby crying somewhere above, the scrape of Sami's shoes on grit.

*[ACTING NOTE: This is the hour. Sami does not know that Ahmad is already dead. The player does not know either, not yet. Everything that happens in this scene is ordinary life under siege, experienced in the gap between a death and the knowledge of it. The dramatic irony is retrospective, it will only become painful when the player replays the scene in their mind after Scene 4.]*

### Sub-scene 3a: The Old Man with Water

**Location:** شارع البير / Well Street: near the communal well
**Time:** 3:25 PM

Sami walks north-east. He passes into a narrower street. Ahead, an **old man** (seventies, thin, in a worn jalabiya) struggles with two heavy plastic jerry cans, yellow, twenty-litre, tied together with rope. He's trying to carry them both but can barely lift one. He sets them down, breathes, tries again.

**[INTERACTION: JERRY CANS]**

> **Arabic:** غالونين مي. عشرين ليتر الواحد. من البير لهون مسافة ربع ساعة. بالحر هاد، ساعة.
> **English:** Two water jugs. Twenty litres each. From the well to here, a fifteen-minute walk. In this heat, an hour.

**[CHOICE A: GAMEPLAY PROMPT]**

> **A1:** ساعدو / Help him: *[Sami approaches the old man and takes one jug]*
> **A2:** ضل ماشي / Keep walking: *[Sami walks past]*

#### Branch A1: Help

*Sami approaches. The old man looks up.*

**الختيار:**
> *(breathing hard)* الله يخلّيك يا ابني...
> *(breathing hard)* God bless you, son...

**سامي:**
> *(taking one jug)* لوين بدك توصّلهن؟
> *(taking one jug)* Where do you need to get these?

**الختيار:**
> هون، ع آخر الزقاق. بنتي وولادها. ما عندهن مي من مبارح.
> *Here, at the end of the alley. My daughter and her children. They've had no water since yesterday.*

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT]** The player walks slowly, carrying the jug. Movement is reduced: Sami leans under the weight. They move down the alley together.

**الختيار:**
> كنت بشيل زيتون من الضيعة، شوالين عكتافي، وما كنت حسّ فيهن. هلّق غالون مي بيكسرني.
> *I used to carry olives from the village, sacks on my shoulders, and feel nothing. Now a jug of water breaks me.*

*They reach a doorway. The old man knocks. A woman's voice from inside. The door opens a crack.*

**الختيار:**
> *(to Sami)* الله يحميك. شو اسمك؟
> *(to Sami)* God protect you. What's your name?

**سامي:**
> سامي.
> *Sami.*

**الختيار:**
> الله يحمي أهلك يا سامي.
> *God protect your family, Sami.*

*The door closes. Sami walks back.*

> **[STATE: compassion +1]**

#### Branch A2: Keep Walking

*Sami walks past. The old man doesn't call out, he expects nothing. Sami's pace doesn't change. The camera lingers on the old man for a moment after Sami passes, then follows Sami.*

*[ACTING NOTE: No judgement from the game. Sami is tired, the heat is brutal, and walking past a struggle is something everyone under siege has done. The player's choice stands without commentary.]*

### Sub-scene 3b: The Mirror

**Location:** A rubble pile near the junction of Well Street and the main road
**Time:** 3:32 PM

Sami passes a collapsed section of wall. Among the debris, broken tiles, a child's sandal, twisted metal, a **shard of mirror** catches the light, flashing.

**[INTERACTION: MIRROR SHARD]**

> **Arabic:** شقفة مراية. بتشوف فيها حالك، أو بتشوف فيها شو ورا الحيطان.
> **English:** A shard of mirror. You can see yourself in it, or you can see what's around walls.

**[TOOL ACQUIRED: MIRROR SHARD]**

> شقفة مراية. بتبيّن فيها الطريق اللي ما عم تشوفها.
> *Mirror shard. It shows the road you can't see.*

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT]** A prompt introduces the mirror mechanic. Ahead, the road passes an intersection exposed to the west: potential sniper angle.

> **[Use mirror]**: استعمل المراية / Use the mirror

The player holds the mirror at the corner. The reflected view shows the intersection: empty, a curtain flapping in a window across the way, no movement. Safe to cross.

*[TUTORIAL: Mirror shard mechanic introduced, hold at corners to see around them before crossing.]*

### Sub-scene 3c: The Walkie-Talkie

**Location:** Near a spotter's post: a half-destroyed upper floor of a residential building
**Time:** 3:38 PM

Sami passes below a building where a **spotter** sits, a young man, maybe twenty, on the third floor with binoculars and a walkie-talkie, watching the sky. He spots Sami below.

**المرصد / The Spotter:**
> *(calling down)* يا زلمة! وين رايح؟ البراميل عم تنزل عالقبلي.
> *(leaning out of his window)* Hey, man! Where are you headed? The planes are hitting the southern quarter.

*[ACTING NOTE: "The southern quarter", where Ahmad went. The player doesn't know what this means yet. The spotter says it factually; strikes fall on different quarters at different times. It's weather.]*

**سامي:**
> رايح عالشرقي. عالأنابيب.
> *Heading to the eastern quarter. To the pipes.*

**المرصد:**
> خود هاد. أحسنلك.
> *Take this. Better for you.*

*The spotter lowers a walkie-talkie on a rope.*

**[TOOL ACQUIRED: WALKIE-TALKIE]**

> لاسلكي. بتسمع فيه تحذيرات الرصد. ما بيبعت، بس بيستقبل.
> *Walkie-talkie. You hear the spotters' warnings. Receive only.*

**المرصد:**
> الموجة ٣. إذا سمعت "مروحي" أو "حربي"، انبطح. إذا سمعت "فيل"، الله يستر.
> *Channel 3. If you hear "mirwahi" (helicopter) or "harbi" (jet), get flat. If you hear "elephant", God help us.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The spotter says "God help us" about the فيل missile not dramatically but with the flat resignation of someone who has seen what it does. The فيل, an enormous improvised rocket, cannot be outrun or hidden from. You hear it and you wait.]*

### Sub-scene 3d: The Jet

**Location:** Open stretch of road between eastern and north-eastern quarters
**Time:** 3:42 PM
**Lighting:** Sun still strong. A clear sky: the worst kind, because a jet comes in low and fast out of a clear sky, and you hear it before you can find it.
**Sound shift:** The walkie-talkie crackles.

**[WALKIE-TALKIE: AUDIO CUE]**

> **لاسلكي:** *(static)* ...حربي... حربي فوق الشرقي... حربي...
> **Walkie-talkie:** *(static)* ...jet... jet over the eastern quarter... jet...

*The sound builds: a thin turbine whine, rising fast. No shape in the sky yet, only the sound, coming from everywhere at once.*

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT: URGENT]**

> **[Go prone!]**: انبطح! / Get down!

*[TUTORIAL: Prone introduced. The player must go prone immediately. Sami drops flat against the base of a wall, pressing himself into the shadow. The whine becomes a roar.]*

**Sound:** The roar tears overhead in a second. The jet's shadow, a swept-wing shape smeared by its speed, flicks across the street and up the walls. The player sees the jet itself for an instant through a gap in the buildings, then it is gone.

*[ACTING NOTE: The player lies prone, face to the ground, listening to the jet go over and the rumble it leaves behind. This should feel long, five or six real seconds of gameplay where the player can do nothing but lie there and listen. The helplessness is the lesson.]*

The rumble rolls away. A beat.

**Sound:** Then, from the south: a deep, concussive boom. The ground shakes slightly. A missile, on another quarter. A plume of grey-brown dust rises above the rooftops in the distance, catching the light.

The walkie-talkie crackles again:

> **لاسلكي:** *(static)* ...ضربة حربي... عالحارة القبلية... ضربة حربي...
> **Walkie-talkie:** *(static)* ...airstrike... the southern quarter... airstrike...

*Sami stands. He brushes dust from his shirt. He keeps walking. He does not react visibly. This is not bravery; this is Tuesday.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The strike fell on the southern quarter, where Ahmad went. The player won't register this now. It matters later.]*

### Sub-scene 3e: The Children and the Shelling

**Location:** Open area near a partially collapsed school building: north-eastern quarter
**Time:** 3:50 PM
**Lighting:** The sun has shifted further. Long shadows from the school building. The light is warmer, starting its turn toward late afternoon amber. Dust from the distant strike hangs in the air like a filter.
**Sound:** Relative quiet after the jet. Children's voices: laughter, argument, the universal noise of play.

A group of **five children** (ages 7–12) play in the open area in front of the school. They're not playing a children's game, they're collecting **scrap metal** from the collapsed wing, sorting it into piles. Rebar, copper wire, aluminium fragments. But they do it with the energy of play, racing each other, calling out finds.

One of them is **Layla**, ten, thin, dark hair pulled back, wearing a too-large shirt that might have been her father's. She's methodical where the others are chaotic, weighing pieces in her hand before deciding which pile they belong to.

**[INTERACTION: LAYLA]**

*If the player approaches Layla:*

**ليلى:**
> *(not looking up)* بتعرف قدّيش كيلو حديد لازم الواحد يلمّ لحتى يشتري كيس برغل؟
> *(not looking up)* Do you know how many kilos of scrap metal you need to collect for a bag of bulgur?

**سامي:**
> كم؟
> *How many?*

**ليلى:**
> كتير.
> *A lot.*

*[ACTING NOTE: She doesn't know the exact number. She knows it's a lot. She's ten.]*

**[INTERACTION: SCRAP METAL PILE]**

> **Arabic:** كومة حديد. نحاس. ألمنيوم. كانت بناية. هلّق هي عملة.
> **English:** A pile of iron. Copper. Aluminium. It was a building. Now it's currency.

**[INTERACTION: COLLAPSED SCHOOL WING]**

> **Arabic:** جناح المدرسة الشرقي. انهار بقصف هاون قبل شهرين. ما كان فيه حدا. الولاد كانوا بالقبو.
> **English:** The eastern wing of the school. Collapsed from mortar fire two months ago. No one was inside. The children were in the basement.

### THE SHELLING

**Sound shift:** A new sound: a sharp, rising whistle. Mortar incoming. Then impact, a crash, closer than the airstrike. A block away, maybe two. The ground shakes. Dust cascades from the damaged buildings.

*The children freeze. Then another whistle. Another impact, closer still. The pattern is walking toward them.*

**Sound:** Multiple mortars now, overlapping. The rhythm is irregular: thud, silence, thud-thud, silence, thud. Each impact throws up dust. Car alarms (from dead cars, triggered by the shockwave) add to the noise.

**[GAMEPLAY BEAT: CHOICE B]**

*The children scatter, most of them running toward the school basement entrance (a reinforced stairwell). Layla and one younger boy are further away, near the scrap pile, exposed. They're frozen, the boy is crying.*

> **B1:** احمي الولاد / Shield the children: *[Sami runs to Layla and the boy, covers them, guides them toward shelter]*
> **B2:** تخبّى / Take cover: *[Sami dives behind the nearest wall]*

#### Branch B1: Shield the Children

**[GAMEPLAY SEQUENCE]** The player runs toward Layla and the boy. A mortar lands in the open area behind them: BOOM, debris flies. The player reaches them.

**سامي:**
> *(grabbing their arms)* يلا يلا يلا! عالدرج!
> *(grabbing their arms)* Go go go! To the stairs!

*Sami pulls them toward the school basement. Another impact, closer. The wall to their left cracks. They're running crouched, Sami's body between the children and the open sky.*

They reach the basement stairs. Other children are already there, pressed against the walls. Sami pushes Layla and the boy in and follows. They crouch together in the stairwell. Dust pours in from above.

**Sound:** Two more impacts, then the shelling shifts: the rhythm moving away, targeting a different area. The sounds grow more distant. The car alarms continue.

In the stairwell, breathing. Layla is pressed against the wall, eyes closed. The younger boy has stopped crying, he's gone quiet, which is worse. Sami's hands are shaking; he presses them flat against his knees.

**ليلى:**
> *(quietly, eyes still closed)* شكراً.
> *(quietly, eyes still closed)* Thank you.

**سامي:**
> *(breathing hard)* ابقوا هون. لا تطلعوا لحتى يهدا.
> *(breathing hard)* Stay here. Don't come out until it's quiet.

> **[STATE: compassion +1, children_helped = true]**

#### Branch B2: Take Cover

*Sami dives behind the nearest wall, a waist-high section of collapsed masonry. He presses flat. Mortar impacts continue. Through the dust, he can see Layla pulling the younger boy by the arm, running toward the basement on her own. They make it. Sami watches from behind his wall.*

*The shelling continues. Then shifts, and fades.*

*Sami stands. He's covered in grey dust. He looks toward the basement entrance. The children are safe, they got there without him. But the knowledge of the choice sits in the body.*

### Sub-scene 3f: After the Shelling: The Cat

**Location:** Same area: in front of the damaged school, after the shelling
**Time:** 3:58 PM
**Lighting:** The dust from the shelling hangs in the air like fog, turning the late-afternoon light diffuse and golden-grey. Visibility is reduced. Everything looks soft-edged, almost peaceful, which is a lie.
**Sound:** The shelling has stopped. Ringing silence: the kind that follows loud noise, where the ears are still adjusting. Then: settling rubble (clicks, trickles of dust). A dog barking far away. Slowly, the ordinary sounds return.

Sami moves out of the stairwell / from behind his cover. The open area has a new crater, fresh, smoking, maybe four metres across. The scrap metal the children were collecting is scattered.

On a **section of collapsed wall**, at eye level, sits a **cat**. A thin tabby with a torn left ear and visible ribs. It is sitting perfectly still, watching Sami. Its eyes are amber.

**[INTERACTION: THE CAT]**

*The player approaches. The cat doesn't move. It blinks slowly, once.*

> **Arabic:** بسّة. مو جوعانة أكتر من أي حدا هون. وعم تستنى نفس الشي: إنو يخلص.
> **English:** A cat. No hungrier than anyone else here. Waiting for the same thing: for it to end.

*The cat holds Sami's gaze for a long moment. Then it turns, drops off the wall, and disappears into the rubble without sound.*

*[ACTING NOTE: No cuteness. No sentimentality. The cat is an animal surviving in a war zone. The encounter is a rest, two living creatures acknowledging each other with no demands, no language, no need. The player should feel a brief, strange peace.]*

**Sound:** A moment of near-silence. Wind. Then the ambient sounds rebuild.

### Sub-scene 3g: The Flashback: Before

**Location:** Memory: triggered by passing the school building
**Time:** Present: 4:00 PM. Memory: an evening in 2010, before the revolution.

*Sami walks past the intact wing of the school. Through a ground-floor window, he sees the empty classroom, chairs stacked, a blackboard, the faded remains of a timetable on the wall. Something about the light through the window, the angle, the colour, triggers the memory.*

**[FLASHBACK: VISUAL SHIFT]**

*The lighting changes: the colour palette warms and softens. The damage disappears. The school is whole. The street is whole. It is an evening in the town before the siege, before the revolution, before anything broke.*

**[FLASHBACK: SOUND SHIFT]**

*The ambient sound transforms: traffic sounds (cars, a distant horn), voices, normal conversation, laughter, someone calling a child in for dinner, a television playing through an open window (an Egyptian soap opera). No gunfire. No generators. The sounds of a town that has electricity, water, shops, people who walk without checking the sky.*

*Sami and Ahmad are younger, twenty-three, twenty-four. They sit in a small garden behind someone's house. A **grape arbour** overhead, heavy with ripe fruit. Glasses of tea on a low table. The light is the warm orange of late summer sunset.*

**أحمد:**
> *(picking a grape, eating it)* بتعرف شو بدي أعمل لمّا خلّص تدريب المعلمين؟
> *(picking a grape, eating it)* You know what I'm going to do when I finish teacher training?

**سامي:**
> شو؟
> *What?*

**أحمد:**
> بدي افتح مدرسة. مو مدرسة حكومية، مدرسة خاصة. ببيت. غرفة وحدة، كراسي وطبشور، وكتب كتير.
> *I'm going to open a school. Not a government school. A private one. In a house. One room, chairs, chalk, and lots of books.*

**سامي:**
> *(amused)* وبتعلّم فيها شعر.
> *(amused)* And you'll teach poetry.

**أحمد:**
> بعلّم فيها كل شي. قواعد وشعر وعلوم وكيف الواحد يفكّر لحالو.
> *I'll teach everything. Grammar and poetry and science and how to think for yourself.*

*A pause.*

**سامي:**
> كيف الواحد يفكّر لحالو. بهالبلد.
> *How to think for yourself. In this country.*

*The mood shifts. Ahmad's face changes. He lowers his voice, instinctively, automatically, the way everyone does.*

**أحمد:**
> *(quietly)* هلّق لا. بس مو لازم يضل هيك.
> *(quietly)* Not now. But it doesn't have to stay this way.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The lowered voice is the pre-revolution reflex. Even in a private garden, among friends, you lower your voice when you say something true. The walls listen. The mukhabarat have informants everywhere. This is the unspoken terror: not a single dramatic event but a lifetime of calibrating every word, every glance, every silence. Ahmad's quiet defiance, "it doesn't have to stay this way", is the seed of everything that follows. He says it like a man testing the weight of a locked door.]*

*Sami doesn't respond. He looks at the grape arbour. A bird lands on a wire above them and sings.*

*The light changes. The bird's song distorts. The sound of the garden fades, replaced by dust, wind, distance.*

**[END FLASHBACK]**

*Sami is standing in front of the damaged school. The window is broken. The classroom is empty. The grape arbour is rubble. The bird is a pigeon, and it flies away when a piece of debris shifts.*

*Sami blinks. He breathes. He walks on.*

**Sound:** The present reasserts itself. Distant shelling. A walkie-talkie crackle. The world he's in.*

### Sub-scene 3h: The Car Battery

**Location:** A ground-floor room of an apartment building, door open to the street
**Time:** 4:03 PM

Sami passes an open door. Inside, a group of three people sit around a **car battery** on a table, charging phones. A tangle of cables. A teenage boy monitors the connections, swapping phones in and out. A woman waits, arms folded.

**[INTERACTION: CAR BATTERY]**

> **Arabic:** بطارية سيارة. بتشحن عشر تلفونات إذا بدها. مصدر الكهربا الوحيد لنص الحارة. الصبي اسمو فادي، عمرو خمستعش، صار المسؤول عنها لأنو هوّي الوحيد اللي بيعرف يفكّ ويركّب.
> **English:** A car battery. It can charge ten phones if it has to. The only source of electricity for half the neighbourhood. The boy is Fadi, fifteen. He became responsible for it because he's the only one who knows how to wire it.

*[ACTING NOTE: This is not a plot moment. It's texture, the infrastructure of siege life. The player sees ordinary people solving ordinary problems with extraordinary patience. The woman waiting for her phone to charge might be trying to call a relative. The boy's competence is a kind of quiet heroism. Sami walks past.]*


## SCENE 4: THE NEWS

**Location:** The corner of Zeitoun Street and the northern road: a small open area where two streets meet, with a low wall and a dead olive tree
**Time:** 4:08 PM
**Lighting:** Late afternoon. The sun is lower now, the light turning amber-gold. Long shadows from the buildings stretch across the street. The light is beautiful: the kind of light that photographers wait for, and the beauty is indifferent to everything.
**Ambient sound:** Quieter than before. The shelling has stopped for now. Voices: a small group of people gathered at the corner, speaking in low tones. The quality of the voices is wrong. The player should feel it before understanding it: something has changed in the texture of the sound.

### Gameplay

Sami approaches the corner. A **group of six or seven people** stand in a loose cluster. Some are looking south. A woman is sitting on the kerb, her face in her hands. A man is on a walkie-talkie, speaking urgently but too quietly to hear.

**أبو يزن** is among them. He sees Sami approaching. His face changes: not dramatically, but the life goes out of it, replaced by something careful, something prepared.

*[ACTING NOTE: Abu Yazan's face is the first signal. He's done this before, delivered news, received news. He knows the weight of the next thirty seconds. He doesn't rush toward Sami. He lets Sami come to him, because the walk toward is the last few seconds of not knowing, and he won't take them from him.]*

### Dialogue

*Sami reaches the group. Abu Yazan steps toward him.*

**أبو يزن:**
> *(quietly, steady)* سامي.
> *(quietly, steady)* Sami.

**سامي:**
> شو في يا عمّو؟ ليش هالناس مجتمعين؟
> *What is it, uncle? Why are these people gathered?*

*Abu Yazan puts a hand on Sami's shoulder. Not heavily, just to anchor him.*

**أبو يزن:**
> *(looking at Sami directly)* أحمد... الله يرحمو يا ابني.
> *(looking at Sami directly)* Ahmad... God have mercy on him, my son.

*[ACTING NOTE: Abu Yazan says it simply. "الله يرحمو", the phrase Muslims use for the dead. Four words. No preamble, no softening. Abu Yazan respects Sami enough to say it plainly, because wrapping it in caution would be worse. His hand stays on Sami's shoulder.]*

*Silence. Sami's face doesn't change immediately. The information enters but hasn't landed. The body hears it before the mind does.*

**سامي:**
> *(not yet understanding)* شو... شو قلت؟
> *(not yet understanding)* What... what did you say?

**أبو يزن:**
> كان عم يقطع شارع المدرسة. قنّاص. ما حسّ بشي. كانت سريعة.
> *He was crossing School Street. A sniper. He didn't feel anything. It was fast.*

*[ACTING NOTE: "He didn't feel anything. It was fast.", Abu Yazan says this as a kindness. He doesn't know if it's true. It's what you say.]*

*Sami's hand goes to the wall beside him. Not reaching for support, just needing to touch something solid. His face is still processing. The sounds of the street, the voices, the walkie-talkie, the wind, seem to grow distant, as if heard through water.*

**سامي:**
> *(very quietly)* إمتى؟
> *(very quietly)* When?

**أبو يزن:**
> من نص ساعة. ساعة بالكتير.
> *Half an hour ago. An hour at most.*

*[ACTING NOTE: Half an hour. While Sami was helping the old man with water, or not. While he was lying flat under a jet. While a cat blinked at him. Ahmad was already gone. The hour of unknowing collapses. Every moment the player just lived through is retroactively stained. The ice cream, the joke, the half-wave, all of it happened in a world where Ahmad was either alive or already dead, and Sami will never know which moment was which.]*

*The woman on the kerb is crying quietly. The man with the walkie-talkie has stopped talking and is looking at Sami. Everyone in the group knows. They have been waiting to see who would tell him.*

**Sound:** The ambient sound pulls back. A ringing tone: not literal tinnitus but an emotional flattening, as if the world's volume has been turned down. The player should feel the contraction of Sami's awareness to a single point.

### CHOICE C: The Main Branch

*A long beat. Sami stands, hand on the wall. Abu Yazan's hand still on his shoulder. The light is gold and long-shadowed and beautiful.*

*Three options appear:*

> **C1:** بدي شوفو. / I need to see him.
> *[Sami pulls away from Abu Yazan and takes a step south, toward the frontline, toward School Street, toward where Ahmad fell.]*

> **C2:** مين عمل هيك؟ / Who did this?
> *[Sami turns to the man with the walkie-talkie. His voice is controlled. He wants information, position, timing, what was seen.]*

> **C3:** *(سكوت)* / *(Silence)*
> *[Sami says nothing. His legs give way, not dramatically, just a slow folding. He sits down on the kerb, in the exact spot where the woman was sitting. She moves to make room. He stares at the ground.]*

### Branch C1: "I need to see him"

**سامي:**
> *(pulling away, voice tight)* بدي شوفو. وينو هلّق؟
> *(pulling away, voice tight)* I need to see him. Where is he now?

**أبو يزن:**
> سامي، ما فيك تروح. الشارع مكشوف. القنّاص لسا هناك.
> *Sami, you can't go. The street is exposed. The sniper is still there.*

**سامي:**
> *(already walking)* وينو.
> *(already walking)* Where is he.

**أبو يزن:**
> *(calling after him)* شارع المدرسة، قبل المفرق. سامي... سامي! ما فيك تروح هلّق!
> *(calling after him)* School Street, before the junction. Sami... Sami! You can't go there now!

*Sami walks south. The group watches him go.*

> **[STATE: grief_response = action, path = retrieval, courage +1]**
> **[END ACT 1: TRANSITION TO ACT 2a: RETRIEVAL]**

### Branch C2: "Who did this?"

**سامي:**
> *(turning to the man with the walkie-talkie, voice flat)* مين عمل هيك. من وين إجت الطلقة.
> *(turning to the man with the walkie-talkie, voice flat)* Who did this. Where did the shot come from.

**زلمة اللاسلكي / Walkie-talkie man:**
> القنّاص عالتلّة الغربية. بالبناية اللي عليها العلم، بتعرفو. نفس القنّاص اللي من أسبوع.
> *The sniper on the western hill. In the building with the flag. You know it. Same sniper from last week.*

**سامي:**
> في حدا شافو وهوّي واقع؟
> *Did anyone see him fall?*

**زلمة اللاسلكي:**
> أم سعيد، هيّي اللي طلعت عليه. لحقوها الشباب بعدها. بس ما قدروا يجرّوه، الشارع مكشوف.
> *Um Said. She's the one who went to him. Some of the men followed her. But they couldn't move him. The street is exposed.*

*[ACTING NOTE: Sami's grief expresses as control. He gathers facts. He organizes information. This is how an engineer processes the unbearable, by turning it into a problem with parameters.]*

**سامي:**
> يعني لسا هناك.
> *So he's still there.*

*Silence from the group. That's the answer.*

> **[STATE: grief_response = witness, path = witness]**
> **[END ACT 1: TRANSITION TO ACT 2b: WITNESS]**

### Branch C3: Silence

*Sami says nothing. His hand slides down the wall. His knees bend, slowly, not a collapse, but a controlled lowering, like a building settling. He sits on the kerb.*

*Abu Yazan watches. He doesn't speak. He sits down beside Sami, not close enough to crowd him, close enough to be there.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The silence is the scene. Sami's face is still. He is looking at the ground between his feet. The other people in the group drift, some leave, giving space; the woman touches his shoulder as she passes. The light moves. A shadow extends over them. The walkie-talkie crackles with something routine and distant.]*

*Time passes. The screen darkens slightly at the edges, time compression. Thirty seconds of real time, representing minutes. The shadows grow longer.*

**أبو يزن:**
> *(gently, after a long silence)* بدك مي؟
> *(gently, after a long silence)* Do you want some water?

*Sami shakes his head. Almost imperceptibly.*

*More silence. Then:*

**أبو يزن:**
> *(not asking Sami to do anything, just speaking into the silence)* أمو بعدها ما بتعرف.
> *(not asking Sami to do anything, just speaking into the silence)* His mother doesn't know yet.

*Sami closes his eyes.*

> **[STATE: grief_response = withdrawal, path = grief, isolation +1]**
> **[END ACT 1: TRANSITION TO ACT 2c: GRIEF]**

## Act 1: Technical Summary

### Duration
- Scene 1 (The Walk): 4–5 minutes
- Scene 2 (The Parting): 1–2 minutes
- Scene 3 (The Hour): 8–10 minutes
- Scene 4 (The News): 2–3 minutes
- **Total: 15–20 minutes**

### Controls Introduced
1. Walk (Scene 1)
2. Examine / interact (Scene 1)
3. Crouch (Scene 1, sniper curtain)
4. Jump / climb (Scene 1, rubble)
5. Go prone (Scene 3d, the jet)
6. Use tool: mirror shard (Scene 3b)
7. Use tool: walkie-talkie (Scene 3c/3d, passive, audio)

### Tools Acquired
1. Mirror shard (Scene 3b)
2. Walkie-talkie (Scene 3c)

### Choices
| Choice | Options | State Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A** (Water) | A1: Help / A2: Pass | compassion ±1 |
| **B** (Shelling) | B1: Shield children / B2: Take cover | compassion ±1, children_helped |
| **C** (The News) | C1: Rush / C2: Seek / C3: Freeze | grief_response, path, courage/isolation |

### Key Emotional Beats
1. The ordinary friendship in the walk, ice cream, children, grammar
2. The casual parting, "tonight, you'll hear the joke"
3. The hour of unknowing, living in a world where Ahmad may already be dead
4. The news, Abu Yazan's four words
5. The choice, action, witness, or withdrawal

### Music / Score Notes
- **Scenes 1–2:** No score. Diegetic sound only. The friendship carries itself.
- **Scene 3a–3f:** Minimal score: a single held note (oud or ney) fading in and out beneath the action, never overwhelming the ambient sound.
- **Scene 3g (Flashback):** A different palette, warmer, fuller. Maybe the ghost of a melody. It cuts off sharply at the end of the flashback.
- **Scene 4 (The News):** No score. Ambient sound pulls back. The silence is the music. Only after Choice C, as the screen begins to fade, does a single ney note enter, sustained, unresolved, and carry into the darkness.
