**Full production script — 15–20 minutes of play per path**
**Time:** 4:30 PM – 7:00 PM, 14 August 2014
**Setting:** The besieged town — southern and eastern quarters

*Act 2 branches into three paths based on Choice C. Each path is a self-contained sequence with its own encounters, choices, and emotional register. The paths diverge fully but converge at certain shared waypoints in Act 3.*

## PATH A: RETRIEVAL — طريق الاسترداد

*From Choice C1: "I need to see him."*

### Scene 2A-1: The Southern Streets

**Location:** الحارة الجنوبية / The southern quarter — residential streets approaching the frontline
**Time:** 4:35 PM
**Lighting:** The amber light of late afternoon. Long shadows stretch across the streets from west to east. The buildings here are more heavily damaged — entire façades have collapsed, leaving rooms exposed like open shelves. Furniture still inside: a sofa, a kitchen table, a child's bed on the third floor with the wall gone. The sun paints it gold.
**Ambient sound:** Closer gunfire than before — single shots, exchanges. The walkie-talkie crackles periodically. Rubble shifts under Sami's feet. A dog howling somewhere ahead.

*[ACTING NOTE: Sami is walking fast, purpose in his stride. Grief is expressing as forward motion. He isn't thinking clearly — he's moving toward Khaled the way you move toward someone who's called your name. The body hasn't accepted what the mind was told.]*

### Gameplay

**[GAMEPLAY — TRAVERSAL SEQUENCE]**

The southern quarter is more dangerous than the eastern. The streets are narrower, more rubble-choked. Snipers have lines of sight into several intersections. The player must:

1. **Use the mirror shard** at two consecutive corners to check for movement before crossing.
2. **Crouch** along a low wall where a building has been flattened — only the ground-floor wall remains, waist-high, running forty metres along the street. Sami must crouch-walk its entire length.
3. **Go prone** through a gap in a collapsed wall where the opening is barely shoulder-width. The player crawls through. Concrete scrapes overhead.

**[WALKIE-TALKIE — AUDIO CUE]**

> **لاسلكي:** *(static)* ...حركة عالحاجز الغربي... احترسوا...
> **Walkie-talkie:** *(static)* ...movement at the western checkpoint... be careful...

### Sub-scene 2A-1b: The Field Hospital

**Location:** A ground-floor apartment converted to a medical station
**Time:** 4:42 PM

Sami passes the **field hospital**. It's a ground-floor flat with the front wall partially open — a tarp hangs where the balcony door used to be, flapping in the wind. Through the gap: a bare room, a camping table with medical supplies, a drip stand improvised from a coat rack. The generator inside is louder here — it powers the single light and the small fridge that keeps medication cool.

A **medic** — a young man, late twenties, stubble, dark circles, wearing a once-white coat now grey — steps out through the tarp. He leans against the exterior wall, pulls out a cigarette, lights it. His hands are steady. His eyes are not.

**[INTERACTION — FIELD HOSPITAL]**

> **Arabic:** نقطة طبية. طبيب واحد ومسعف. بداوو كل شي من جروح القنّاص لحالات الجفاف. ما عندهن بنج كافي. ساعات بيضطرّوا يخيّطو بلا بنج.
> **English:** A medical point. One doctor and a paramedic. They treat everything from sniper wounds to dehydration. They don't have enough anaesthetic. Sometimes they stitch without it.

The medic notices Sami.

**المسعف / The Medic:**
> *(exhaling smoke)* وين رايح يا أخي؟ الجنوبي ما في فيه شي.
> *(exhaling smoke)* Where are you going, brother? There's nothing in the south.

**سامي:**
> صاحبي هناك.
> *My friend is there.*

**المسعف:**
> *(pause, understanding)* خالد؟
> *Khaled?*

*Sami nods.*

**المسعف:**
> *(drops the cigarette, steps on it)* الله يرحمو. ما قدرنا نوصلّو.
> *(drops the cigarette, steps on it)* God rest his soul. We couldn't reach him.

*[ACTING NOTE: "We couldn't reach him" — the medic says this like a confession. The field hospital's job is to reach people. They couldn't. The shame of helplessness in a place where helplessness is the constant.]*

**المسعف:**
> الشارع مكشوف. ما حدا بيقدر يقطع من هناك.
> *The street is exposed. No one can cross there.*

**سامي:**
> بدي شوف.
> *I need to see.*

*The medic watches Sami walk past. He lights another cigarette.*

### Scene 2A-2: The No-Man's-Land

**Location:** The edge of شارع المدرسة / School Street — the frontline area
**Time:** 4:55 PM
**Lighting:** The sun is lower, angled. The street ahead is fully lit — no shadows, no cover. The no-man's-land is a stretch of about sixty metres of open road between two clusters of buildings. On the far side, partially visible: the regime checkpoint — sandbags, a vehicle, a flagpole with a flag.
**Ambient sound:** Near-silence. This is too close to the frontline for ambient noise. The absence of sound is itself a sound — the held breath of a place where being heard means being targeted. A single bird call. Nothing else.

### Gameplay

Sami reaches the edge of the no-man's-land. He is behind the last intact wall before the open street. Ahead, the road stretches to the checkpoint. In the middle of the road — visible from here, about thirty metres out — a shape on the ground. Covered. Someone has thrown a blanket or a sheet over it. Khaled.

*[ACTING NOTE: The shape on the ground. The player sees it. The blanket is dark — grey or brown — and it lies with the stillness of something that will never move again. No detail is visible. The distance is a mercy.]*

**[INTERACTION — THE SHAPE ON THE ROAD]**

> **Arabic:** شكل مغطّى بحرام، وسط الشارع. لا بقدر وصلّو، ولا بقدر بعد نظري عنو.
> **English:** A shape covered with a blanket, in the middle of the street. I can't reach him, and I can't look away.

**رجل قريب / A man nearby** (crouching behind the same wall — a local fighter, unarmed, watching):

**الرجل:**
> *(whispering)* إنت سامي؟ صاحب خالد؟
> *(whispering)* Are you Sami? Khaled's friend?

**سامي:**
> إيه.
> *Yeah.*

**الرجل:**
> أم سعيد هيي يلي غطّتو. طلعت عليه وهي عارفة إنو القنّاص عم يرصدها. حطّت الحرام وبعدين رجعت. ما رماها. ما بعرف ليش.
> *Um Said is the one who covered him. She went out knowing the sniper was watching. She put the blanket over him and came back. He didn't shoot her. I don't know why.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The sniper didn't shoot Um Said. Maybe respect. Maybe his scope was elsewhere for a moment. Maybe he doesn't shoot women. Maybe he just didn't feel like it. The arbitrariness is part of the horror — life and death decided by a stranger's whim.]*

**الرجل:**
> ما فينا نجيبو. الشارع مكشوف. القنّاص بالمبنى يلي فيه العلم — عالتلّة. بيشوف كل شي.
> *We can't get him. The street is exposed. The sniper is in the building with the flag — on the hill. He can see everything.*

Sami stares at the shape. The light is golden on it. The blanket ripples slightly in a breeze.

## PATH B: WITNESS — طريق الشاهد

*From Choice C2: "Who did this?"*

### Scene 2B-1: The Witnesses

**Location:** الحارة الجنوبية / The southern quarter — near where it happened, but at safe distance
**Time:** 4:35 PM
**Lighting:** The same amber light, but here the buildings cast longer shadows. The southern quarter is quieter — people have retreated indoors after the sniper incident. A few figures move between doorways, quick and low.
**Ambient sound:** Muted. Indoor voices through walls. A child coughing. The walkie-talkie, barely audible.

*[ACTING NOTE: Sami's grief is expressing as method. He is gathering information because information is something you can hold. He moves through the neighbourhood with the controlled focus of someone who has decided to understand before he can feel.]*

### Gameplay

Sami walks into the southern quarter. He is looking for witnesses — people who saw what happened. The gameplay is conversational: walking to different locations and talking to people. Between conversations, the player navigates streets using crouch and mirror mechanics.

### Sub-scene 2B-1a: Um Said

**Location:** A ground-floor apartment, door ajar, curtains drawn
**Time:** 4:40 PM

A woman — **Um Said**, fifties, thick arms, a face weathered by sun and worry — sits just inside her front door. She's the one who went out into the no-man's-land to cover Khaled's body. People in the street directed Sami here.

**سامي:**
> *(at the doorway)* أم سعيد؟
> *(at the doorway)* Um Said?

**أم سعيد:**
> *(looking up)* ادخل. ما تضل بالشارع.
> *(looking up)* Come in. Don't stay in the street.

*Sami enters. The room is dim — one window, curtains drawn. A mattress on the floor. A tray with a teapot and glasses, cold.*

**سامي:**
> إنتي طلعتي عليه.
> *You went out to him.*

**أم سعيد:**
> إي.
> *Yes.*

**سامي:**
> ليش؟ القنّاص كان هناك.
> *Why? The sniper was there.*

**أم سعيد:**
> *(flatly)* لأنو ما بيصير يضل هيك بالشمس. حرام. ما حدا بيستاهل يضل مكشوف هيك.
> *(flatly)* Because he can't stay like that in the sun. It's wrong. No one deserves to lie there exposed like that.

*[ACTING NOTE: Um Said's act was not heroic in her own view. It was necessary. There was a body in the street and the sun was on it and that was not acceptable. She went. The sniper didn't fire. She came back. She does not discuss the sniper's reasoning because it's irrelevant to her.]*

**سامي:**
> شو... شو شفتي؟
> *What... what did you see?*

**أم سعيد:**
> *(carefully)* كان ماشي عشارع المدرسة. وحدو. ما كان راكض — كان ماشي عادي. سمعت الطلقة وبعدها شفتو. ما حسّ بشي.
> *(carefully)* He was walking down School Street. Alone. He wasn't running — just walking normally. I heard the shot and then I saw him. He didn't feel anything.

*A pause.*

**أم سعيد:**
> كانت شنطتو وقعت جنبو. سحبتها لمّا طلعت. هون.
> *His bag had fallen next to him. I pulled it away when I went out. It's here.*

*She reaches behind her and produces a **cloth bag** — Khaled's school bag. Inside: a few exercise books, a pen, and a **camera** — a small digital camera, old model, with a cracked screen.*

**[INTERACTION — KHALED'S BAG]**

> **Arabic:** شنطة خالد. دفاتر الولاد يلي كان عم يصحّحها. قلم أزرق. وكاميرا — كان يصوّر فيها كل شي. قال: "لازم حدا يحكي قصتنا."
> **English:** Khaled's bag. The children's exercise books he was marking. A blue pen. And a camera — he used it to photograph everything. He said: "Someone has to tell our story."

**أم سعيد:**
> خودها. هيي إلك أكتر مني.
> *Take it. It's yours more than mine.*

**[TOOL ACQUIRED: CAMERA]**

> كاميرا خالد. شاشة مكسورة بس لسا شغّالة. آخر صورة فيها: أولاد بالصف، عم يكتبوا بدفاترهن.
> *Khaled's camera. Cracked screen but still working. Last photo on it: children in class, writing in their exercise books.*

**أم سعيد:**
> *(looking at Sami)* أمو بتعرف؟

### Sub-scene 2B-1b: The Baker

**Location:** A courtyard behind a building — improvised bakery
**Time:** 5:05 PM

Sami walks through the neighbourhood. On the way to finding more witnesses, or simply walking to avoid standing still, he passes through a **courtyard** where a man — **Abu Firas**, sixties, thick forearms, flour dust in his beard — operates a **hand mill** and a **clay oven** built from rubble and salvaged bricks.

The smell of baking bread fills the courtyard. It's a grey, dense bread — made from ground animal feed — but the smell is warm, and in the siege, warmth is rare.

**[INTERACTION — CLAY OVEN]**

> **Arabic:** فرن طين مبني من حجار وباطون. أبو فراس بناه بإيدو. بيخبز ست أرغفة كل ساعة. الطحين مش طحين — علف حيوانات مطحون. الخبز رمادي وخشن. بس ساخن.
> **English:** A clay oven built from stones and concrete. Abu Firas built it by hand. He bakes six loaves an hour. The flour isn't flour — it's ground animal feed. The bread is grey and rough. But it's hot.

Abu Firas sees Sami and tears a piece of bread from a cooling loaf. He holds it out.

**أبو فراس:**
> كل. إنت شكلك ما أكلت اليوم.
> *Eat. You look like you haven't eaten today.*

**سامي:**
> *(taking it)* شكراً يا عمّو.
> *(taking it)* Thank you, uncle.

**أبو فراس:**
> *(working the hand mill, grinding)* بتعرف قديش كان سعر كيلو الطحين قبل الحصار؟ خمسين ليرة. هلّق — لو بتلاقي طحين حقيقي — عشرين ألف. وهاد مش طحين. هاد أكل بقر.
> *(working the hand mill, grinding)* You know how much a kilo of flour cost before the siege? Fifty liras. Now — if you can find real flour — twenty thousand. And this isn't flour. This is cow feed.

*Sami eats the bread. It's grey, dense, gritty. He chews slowly.*

**أبو فراس:**
> بس بيشبع. وإذا بيشبع — بينفع.
> *But it fills you up. And if it fills you up — it'll do.*

*[ACTING NOTE: The bread is terrible. Sami eats it like it's the best thing he's ever had. Not because of the taste — because someone handed it to him. The act of receiving is what he needed.]*

## PATH C: GRIEF — طريق الحزن

*From Choice C3: Silence.*

### Scene 2C-1: Sitting

**Location:** The corner of Zeitoun Street — where the news was delivered
**Time:** 4:20 PM – 5:00 PM (compressed)
**Lighting:** The amber light deepens slowly. Shadows crawl across the ground. The quality of light changes from warm to elegiac — the sun preparing to leave.
**Ambient sound:** Very quiet. The group has dispersed. Most people have left. The walkie-talkie crackles occasionally from somewhere nearby. A child's voice, indistinct. Wind. The settling sounds of the town.

*[ACTING NOTE: Sami sits on the kerb. Abu Yazan sits beside him. This is a long scene — not in gameplay minutes but in emotional time. The screen darkens at the edges slightly, time compression. The player cannot move. The camera is still. What moves is the light: shadows growing, the colour shifting. The siege continues around them — sounds, a distant crump of a mortar, a helicopter far away — and they sit through it.]*

### Gameplay

**[GAMEPLAY — STILLNESS]**

*For thirty seconds of real time, the player cannot act. Sami sits. Abu Yazan sits. The ambient sound plays. The light moves.*

*Then, slowly, control returns — but only partial. The player can look around. They can examine objects near Sami.*

**[INTERACTION — THE KERB]**

> **Arabic:** رصيف. باطون مكسور. في خربشات عليه — أسماء وتواريخ. حدا كتب: "كنا هون."
